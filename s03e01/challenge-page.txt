CHALLENGE · S03E01
WEEK 3 · DAY 1

status for the night

Product wants a public status page for the night. Six people sent what they want — a memo, a screenshot, a recording, a PDF, a whiteboard photo, a live board. With your harness, turn it into one requirements list, then build the page from that list. Not all of it reaches your harness: some lives on the Challenge page.

YOU DO
You sign in to this page when your harness asks: one of the sources is only here. Where the sources disagree, product decides, not you.
YOUR HARNESS DOES
It turns what several people sent into one requirements list, each requirement cited to its source. Then it builds the status page from that list and deploys it.
YOU NEED
Somewhere to host a public https page with no login, such as Cloudflare Workers.
DONE WHEN
The course accepts the list, then the page your harness deployed from it.

Your harness is your AI agent plus the tools, sign-ins and skills you give it.
