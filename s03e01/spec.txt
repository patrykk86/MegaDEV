What it is

A public page that says what happened to sched-prod during the night, so support can point people
at one link instead of answering the same question again.

It is a page people read and a JSON other systems read. Both say the same thing.


Requirements

Tagged ones are the ones we hold the build to. Untagged lines are context.


   One row per incident: when it opened, when it closed, what it touched.


   R-L2Z — Newest incident at the top.


   R-K5Z — Each incident names who ran it.


   Keep it plain. No login, no tracking, no cookie banner.



Data

Incidents come from the ops feed, on the course site: /challenges/status-for-the-night/api/in
cidents/111cc74e5b37. It is JSON and updates through the night.

The page's own JSON is status.json, beside the page. Its fields: updatedAt (when the data was
read) and incidents (the rows).
Formats

   Times in 24-hour form, HH:MM.


   R-Z2L — Dates are shown day first: 03/11. This is the format the night shift writes in its notes.



Scope

   R-K8X — Last night only. Nothing older stays on the page.


   Out of scope: search, subscriptions.
